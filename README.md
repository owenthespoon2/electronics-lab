# Electronics lab

My journal of learning electronics with the parts I've collected over the years: experiments, small builds, what worked, what didn't, with photos.

## Layout
- `log/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`: one entry per session (what I tried, wiring, photos, what I learned).
- `projects/<name>/`: bigger builds that last more than a day (notes, wiring diagrams, code).
- `parts/`: what I have on hand (the photo inventory lives in my Hub; this is a short summary).
